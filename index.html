OpenStory

Knowledge in the form humans cannot put down.人類放唔低手嘅知識形態。

Everyone, besides songs and movies, loves stories. This is the AI era's only remedy — the answer that keeps everyone from getting dumber.除咗歌同電影，人人都鍾意故事。呢個係 AI 時代唯一嘅解藥——令所有人唔好變蠢嘅答案。

What it is呢個係咩

A fact is forgotten; a story is retold — by grandparents, by children, by strangers. OpenStory is the school's narrative engine: every canon subject gets its story form — nursery rhymes and children's tales, science told as adventure, history told as lives — readable by ordinary people, at every age, in every language from day one. Labels survive the telling: legend says so, sourced stories carry their sources, fiction lives proudly labelled. And OpenStory distils every story told into language, logic, lessons and remedies — stories in, distilled canon out.

事實會被遺忘；故事會被重述——由祖父母、由小朋友、由陌生人。OpenStory 係學校嘅敘事引擎：每個 canon 題目都有佢嘅故事形態——童謠同兒童故事、講成冒險嘅科學、講成人命嘅歷史——普通人讀得明，任何年齡，第一日就任何語言。標籤喺講述中保留：傳說就標明傳說，有來源嘅故事帶住來源，小說自豪地標明小說。OpenStory 仲會將每個講出嚟嘅故事蒸餾成語言、邏輯、教訓同解藥——故事入，蒸餾後嘅 canon 出。

END GOAL

Turn what Dawn has made readable — nursery rhymes, children's things, science things, all of it valuable and knowledgeable — into stories worth spreading to the whole earth forever, so people carry the knowledge themselves, in memory, for life.

將 Dawn 已經整到普通人讀得明嘅嘢——童謠、兒童嘅嘢、科學嘅嘢，全部有價值、有知識——變成值得傳遍全地球、傳到永遠嘅故事，令人將知識帶喺自己身上、記一世。
